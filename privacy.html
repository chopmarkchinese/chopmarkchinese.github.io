Privacy policy

Chopmark for Android · Effective 29 October 2026

Chopmark is a Chinese learning app. This policy explains what the app keeps, where, and why. In short: your learning stays on your phone unless you sign in and subscribe to sync it; we have no ads, no advertising trackers and no analytics, and we never sell your data.

What stays on your phone

Your cards, streak, XP, daily history, the name you type in the app and your settings are stored on your phone. If Android backup is on, Google may include them in your device backup under Google's own terms. The sign-in session is left out of that backup.

What we collect, and why

DataWhenWhy

Who processes it

  • Supabase hosts accounts and synced progress.
  • RevenueCat checks subscriptions. It receives purchase information from Google Play and the app user ID; it does not receive your learning progress.
  • Google handles payments through Google Play, Google sign-in if you use it, and crash reports through Google Play's Android vitals when you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics.

These companies process data for us under their own security and privacy terms and may store it outside your country. Data travels between the app and these services over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Text-to-speech

Chinese audio is spoken by your phone's text-to-speech engine. Chopmark sends it only the words to say; the engine's maker decides how it works.

How long we keep it

Account and synced progress are kept until you delete your account. Deleting it removes your account and the progress stored in the cloud straight away; progress on your phone stays until you uninstall the app or use Me › Progress › Reset progress. Google Play keeps its own purchase records; ask us and we will also delete your RevenueCat customer record.

Your choices and rights

  • Use Chopmark without an account; nothing then leaves your phone except subscription checks.
  • Delete your account in the app (Me › Account › Delete account) or as described on this page.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, a correction or a deletion by email. We answer within 30 days.

Children

Chopmark is made for teens and adults and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date above, and tell you in the app when the change matters.

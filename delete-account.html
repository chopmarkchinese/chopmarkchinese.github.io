Delete your Chopmark account

Chopmark Co. · Chopmark: Learn Chinese

In the app

  1. Open Chopmark and go to Me › Account.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Without the app

Email chopmark.chinese@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Chopmark account". We delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, name, profile picture link and account ID.
  • Your progress stored in the cloud: cards, streak, XP and history.
  • On request, your RevenueCat customer record.

What is kept

  • Progress on your phone stays until you uninstall the app or reset it in Me › Progress.
  • Google Play keeps its own purchase records. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions.
